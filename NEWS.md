# News

What each release says in the Discord server's #general channel: one short,
friendly post per version, in plain words for players. Each release PR
writes the new version's section here next to its CHANGELOG.md section, and
after the upload `tools/post_general.py` posts it, with the CurseForge link
added at the end. Each change thanks, by their Discord name, the players
whose feedback or reports led to it; this is the only report that names
players. A version with no section here posts nothing in #general.
This file does not ship in the addon.

## 0.20.0

Hey everyone! Farmer just got an update.

Farmer now learns how long herbs and veins take to grow back, just from your own gathering. After you've come back to a spot a few times, the map tells you when it'll be ready again. Thanks Lottus27 for the idea!

If you use TomTom, the goal arrow now hands its spot to TomTom so you only see one arrow. Thanks Ezmode for asking!

Restart the game fully after you update. Happy farming!
