# Sounds

Made for Blackthornes Farmer by `tools/make_sounds.py`, from plain sine waves.
No sound library was used, so they are the addon's own and ship under its
license.

- `rare-drop.ogg`: an item of green quality or better drops.
- `drop-rates-unlocked.ogg`: a mob's 20th kill, or a fishing spot's 20th cast.
- `rank-up.ogg`: a mob climbs your top 15, or joins it.
- `xp-tick.ogg`: each tenth of a level crossed on the XP bar.
- `top-farm.ogg`: an auction scan puts a new mob first on your board.
- `cute.ogg`, `laurel.ogg`, `slayer.ogg`, `beast.ogg`, `fear.ogg`: small
  surprises along the way while you farm.
